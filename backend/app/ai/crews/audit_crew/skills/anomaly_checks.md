# Skill: Anomaly checks
- BIG_SALE: a single sale much larger than usual. Check: confirm the bill and quantity; maybe a bulk or wedding order.
- COST_JUMP: purchase cost changed more than 20%. Check: supplier invoice, gold rate change, typing mistake.
- PRICE_BELOW_COST: selling price lower than cost. Check: the selling price in the design details.
- FREQUENT_UNDO: many entries undone in a week. Check: whether staff need help with entry.
- SALE_AND_RETURN_SAME_DAY: may be an exchange. Check: whether it was recorded correctly.
- BACKDATED_ENTRIES: entries recorded long after their date. Check: they were intended.
- ZERO_MIN_STOCK: high-demand design without a minimum. Check: set a minimum to get alerts.
