# Skill: Reorder quantity rules
- The baseline target is: pieces sold per month × cover months + minimum stock, minus stock on hand.
- Sold-out HIGH demand designs come first (priority HIGH).
- Festive or wedding season ahead: you may raise quantities for necklaces, bangles and sets, but never above max_qty.
- max_qty reflects how much of a design this shop normally keeps (usual_stock). Small shops keep little;
  respect it, capital is limited. A monthly purchase budget may also trim the final plan.
- Designs with very little history: be conservative.
- Monsoon or a falling trend: you may lower quantities (max −50%).
- Never reorder DEAD designs. Be careful with LOW demand designs.
- Quantities are whole pieces.
