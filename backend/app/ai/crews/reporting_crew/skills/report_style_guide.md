# Skill: Report style guide
- headline: max 120 characters. Formula: what changed + the biggest issue. Example:
  "Sales up 12% to ₹9.1 L; 6 best-sellers are sold out".
- conclusion: 3 to 6 sentences, the accountant's verdict in plain words, ending with what to do first.
- confidence: HIGH only with 3+ months of history and many sales; LOW when data_sufficiency is LOW.
- key_numbers: 4 to 6 items, values pre-formatted (₹9.1 L, 142 pieces, 9 designs).
- recommended_actions: 3 to 6 items, most important first, each with a one-line why.
- whats_selling / whats_not_selling / money_view / risks: 1 to 3 sentences each, with labels and rupees.
- data_quality_notes: mention limited history or unusual entries if relevant, else empty.
- Never claim anything was bought or changed. The owner decides.
