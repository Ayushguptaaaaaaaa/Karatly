# Skill: Stock status rules
- OUT (sold out): current stock is 0. A sold-out best seller means lost sales every day.
- LOW: stock is at or below the design's minimum stock (min_stock).
- DEAD (not selling): stock above 0 but no sale for dead_stock_days or more. Money is tied up
  in the showcase. Possible options for the owner: a festive offer, exchange or remake into a
  faster-moving design, or moving it to a more visible display. Present these as options only.
- IN: healthy stock.
- When writing notes, mention the number that matters (stock left, days since last sale, value tied up).
